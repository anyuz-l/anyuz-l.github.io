隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月 18 日

欢迎使用「公式助手」（FormulaKit，以下称"本 App"）。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

本 App 不收集、不存储任何个人身份信息。您的所有数据（包括公式库、编辑历史、收藏、自定义公式、设置项）都仅保存在您设备的本地存储中（SwiftData），不会上传到任何服务器。

2. 购买与订阅信息

  • 本 App 提供的"年度会员"自动续订订阅（价格以您所在 App Store 地区显示的当地货币为准）与"永久买断"（一次性付费）交易，均由 Apple（App Store）处理支付与扣款。
  • 本 App 仅能获知交易结果（例如是否已购买、订阅是否有效），以决定是否为您解锁 Pro 功能。我们无法获取也无法存储您的支付账户信息（如银行卡、Apple ID 密码等）。
  • 订阅管理（续订、取消、退款）请通过 Apple 官方渠道操作：设备「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」或 App Store。

3. 网络与第三方服务

  • 本 App 的公式渲染（KaTeX）资源以本地资源为主，不依赖外部服务。
  • 本 App 不含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK，不向任何第三方共享数据。
  • 本 App 仅在您使用"保存图片"功能时请求相册的"仅添加"权限（用于将导出的公式图片保存到相册，无法读取您的相册内容）；不请求相机、通讯录、定位等其他权限。

4. 数据共享与出售

我们不会出售、出租或向任何第三方共享您的个人信息。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 政策的更新

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本页面发布更新版本，并更新页首的"最后更新日期"。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Welcome to FormulaKit (the "App"), a LaTeX formula editor. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. All of your data (including formula library, editing history, favorites, custom formulas, and settings) is stored locally on your device only (SwiftData) and is never uploaded to any server.

2. Purchases and Subscriptions

  • The "Yearly Plan" auto-renewable subscription (priced in your local currency as shown in your App Store region) and the "Lifetime" one-time purchase are processed entirely by Apple (App Store).
  • The App only learns the outcome of a transaction (e.g., whether you purchased or whether your subscription is active) in order to unlock Pro features. We cannot access or store your payment details (such as bank cards or Apple ID passwords).
  • Manage your subscription (renewal, cancellation, refund) through official Apple channels: device "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions" or the App Store.

3. Network and Third-Party Services

  • Formula rendering (KaTeX) resources are primarily bundled locally and do not rely on external services.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with any third party.
  • The App requests photo library "add-only" permission only when you use the "Save Image" feature (to save exported formula images; it cannot read your library contents). No camera, contacts, location, or other permissions are requested.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
