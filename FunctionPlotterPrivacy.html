Function Plot Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Welcome to Function Plot (the "App"), a function plotting tool that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, upload, or sell any personally identifiable information. You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, phone number, or email address. Your expressions, formula library, favorites, plotting history, and display settings are stored locally on your device (files and UserDefaults inside the app sandbox) and are never synchronized to any server.

2. Purchases and Subscriptions

  • The "Yearly Membership" auto-renewable subscription (priced in your local currency as shown in your App Store region) and the "Lifetime" one-time purchase are both processed entirely by Apple (App Store).
  • The App only learns the outcome of a transaction (for example, whether a purchase succeeded or whether a subscription is still active) in order to unlock the corresponding features. We cannot access or store your payment details such as bank cards or your Apple ID password.
  • Manage renewals, cancellations, and refunds through official Apple channels: "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions" on your device, or the purchase history page in the App Store.

3. Network and Third-Party Services

  • All expression parsing, formula typesetting, and graph rendering happen on your device. The App does not depend on any remote service.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not share your data with any third party.
  • When you export an image or vector file, the App writes only the file you choose through the system file picker and does not read any other file. The App requests no photo library, camera, contacts, or location permissions.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with any third party.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Plot documents, the formula library, history, and settings stay on your device for as long as you keep them.
  • You can delete all plotting history at any time via "Settings → Clear History", and remove individual formulas from the library.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all of its local data. We keep no copies on any server.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information, using it under a guardian's supervision involves no data transmission.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
